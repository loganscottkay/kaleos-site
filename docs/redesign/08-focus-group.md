# Focus group: KALEOS v4 (synthetic panel)

September 17, 2026. Nine personas, standard format plus one extra slot Ryan asked for (enterprise IT). Read against the live local build at phone and laptop widths.

**What the landing surface promises, in its own words:** "Custom AI solutions for the modern business." Sub: a premium AI implementation practice; agents handle the work and a person you trust signs off. Buttons: "Talk with us" (opens Calendly in a new tab) and "How we work" (scrolls to the orbit). No price anywhere; the tiers table says pricing is quoted on a call. The Assessment form is the last section of /audit. Nearest alternatives: an AI freelancer, an internal hire, a chatbot vendor, or doing nothing. Hardest thing in the first five minutes: knowing what it costs and what "the gate" means.

Verdict spread was fixed before writing: 2 Adopts, 3 Tries then drifts, 2 Bounces, 1 Hostile, 1 Indifferent.

## The so what

**The pattern.** Everyone inside the market understood the offer from the first screen, which is rare. Their stalls were all about proof and money: the pending tags on the testimonials (Priya, Dana), and no price or duration signal before the third scroll (Marcus). The outside slots bounced for the right reasons, which means the positioning is doing its job.

**The one fix.** Put the two facts a buyer wants first on the first screen: "Thirty days to the first system, scoped and priced on a call." That moves Marcus from drifts toward a forward, and it costs one sentence. The second fix is not a design change: no testimonial ships with "Pending confirmation" visible. Dana is right.

**Who this is for.** Owner-operators of services businesses with 10 to 150 people who already feel the cost of manual work. Not enterprise IT, not nonprofits wanting a chatbot. Narrower than "the modern business" in the headline, and that is fine as long as the "Who we work with" section keeps saying so.

**What to check with real humans.** Show three owners the first screen on a phone and ask what it costs and how long it takes. Ask a non-technical office manager to fill the form and watch the outcome field. Reload the home page in front of a designer four times.

These are simulated reactions, useful for finding objections and blind spots, not for deciding whether the idea works. The panel is a list of things to go check.
