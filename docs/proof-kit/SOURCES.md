## Usage guidelines

**Harvard Business School.** Identity guidelines: https://www.hbs.edu/marketing/logos.html. Name and logo policy: https://www.hbs.edu/about/campus-and-culture/policies/use-of-harvard-name-and-logo. The policy says HBS logos are largely prohibited from appearing in an outside company's marketing materials or website, or alongside that company's logo. The shield can only appear in black, crimson, or the crimson and black combination, never altered or redrawn. On kaleoshq.com it may only be used to state a fact about Logan's background (he designed and deployed AI systems at HBS), never in a client wall, never next to the Kaleos logo as a partner, and never in a way that implies HBS endorses Kaleos. If in doubt, use the words "Harvard Business School" as text instead of the shield.

**Claude / Anthropic.** Press kit: https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit. Press contact: press@anthropic.com. Anthropic does not publish a public brand usage page; the press kit is the official source. Keep the logo's proportions, give it clear space, use the Slate version on light grounds and Ivory on dark, and do not recolor it. On kaleoshq.com it represents the tools Kaleos builds with, not a client or a partner endorsement.

**Client logos (Advisor Solutions OS, Bohan Contracting, Cogniify).** These are real clients. Use them only once the matching testimonial in `testimonials.json` is confirmed, since the confirmation also covers permission to show the logo.
